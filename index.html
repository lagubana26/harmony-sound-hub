From a suburban garage to a full music hub

Harmony Sound Hub started in 2010 as a small recording studio, built by musicians and audio engineers who wanted every artist to have access to great, affordable sound.

Over the years we grew from that garage into a multi-facility hub that serves rising talents and established artists alike. We blend the analog warmth of vintage gear with modern digital equipment, so the sound you get is as unique as you are.

Today we are a music store, a repair bench and a teaching space, working with players across every genre.
